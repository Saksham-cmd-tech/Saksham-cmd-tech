### Research & Technical Work

<div align="center">
  <img src="assets/hcts_banner.jpg" width="100%" alt="HCTS Procedural Terrain" />
</div>

<br>

**HCTS (Island Engine)**  
A research-oriented procedural world-generation system. Focuses on high-performance, thermodynamics-driven procedural terrain generation. By implementing advanced geometric algorithms such as Voronoi Tessellation, Delaunay Triangulation, Poisson Disk Sampling, and Lloyd Relaxation, the engine generates structurally sound geological layouts and procedural islands with sophisticated underlying logic.
